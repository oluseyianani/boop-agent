# Rotatable libraries

Rotate across scripts to prevent fatigue. Never reuse the same hook + demographic + setting combo back to back.

## Hook frameworks (fill the blanks)

- `If you are trying to ___ this is how you finally do it without ___.`
- `Your ___ is ___, and ___ won't fix it.`
- `The biggest myth about ___ is...`
- `I tested ___ so you don't have to.`
- `I wish someone had told me this before I started ___.`
- `I found this out by accident while ___.`
- `What nobody warns you about with ___ is...`
- `Why ___ works when nothing else does.`
- `Why your ___ isn't working and how to fix it.`
- `Don't make this mistake with ___.`
- `One thing I'll never do again in ___.`
- `Three mistakes keeping you stuck in ___.`
- `This one thing changed everything for me in ___.`
- `What nobody admits about ___ is...`
- `How I stopped ___.`
- `The truth behind my ___.`
- `If I could go back and tell myself one thing about ___.`

Rambling hooks fail. `"I scrubbed my fridge on Saturday on my hands and knees"` narrates the creator's life. The first words must call out the audience.

**Punchier scroll-stoppers** (good for warm/mid-funnel, TikTok-native): curiosity/secret loops ("Nobody is talking about this, but it changes everything"), controversial hot takes ("Hot take: [common advice] is completely wrong"), hyper-specific scenarios ("If you've ever [oddly specific moment]—this is for you"), transformation tension ("3 years of progress in 30 seconds"). Keep them honest — never promise a feature the product lacks.

## Universal reframe pattern

The hook's paired reframe (absolves the viewer of guilt, sets up the mechanism):

```
"Most people think ___ is a [SURFACE] problem so they keep [SURFACE BEHAVIOR].
But it is actually a [STRUCTURAL/HIDDEN] problem, which is why [SURFACE SOLUTION]
physically cannot reach it."
```

## Soft-CTA bank

- `"I'll leave a link to the one I'm using below."`
- `"I'll drop a link below if you want to look into it."`
- `"Linking it below so you don't have to go searching for it."`
- `"Leaving a link in the comments / bio so you can check it out yourself."`

Default to these for warm audiences. Don't invent new CTA structures unless testing has earned it.

## Caption templates (organic post caption — lowercase, ≤1 soft emoji, no hashtags unless tested, 1–2 sentences, alludes not sells)

- **Confessional / Accidental Discovery:** `"nobody told me ___. wish I had figured this out years ago"`
- **Tested It:** `"spent way too much money on ___ before I figured out why none of it worked"`
- **Myth Buster:** `"if you've been ___ thinking it would ___, read this"`
- **Universal:** `"genuinely did not know this until recently. if you ___ this might explain why nothing's been working for you"`

Put the actual link in the **first comment**, not the caption: `"linking what I'm using in my bio if anyone wants to check it out."`

## Variation types (persona / voice)

| Type | Voice | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Confessional | first-person, present tense, a personal admission | emotional categories (beauty, wellness, parenting, money-stress) |
| Tested It So You Don't Have To | audience surrogate who tried every wrong solution first | crowded categories, many competing products |
| Myth Buster | opens with a corrective reframe | educational categories, audience holds wrong beliefs |
| Accidental Discovery | creator stumbled onto the insight | lowest sales pressure, fellow-explorer framing |
| Animated Infomercial | no creator, product/app b-roll + VO | scaling output without talent, or product is the hero |

## Avatar prompt (GPT Image 2)

One avatar per ad, matched to the pain point's audience — **vary the avatar per problem/solution**, don't reuse one face for everything. Emit as a separate block.

**Template:**
```
Generate a candid, natural photo of [an attractive/relatable] [GENDER] in [her/his]
[AGE RANGE], [VIBE — 2-3 words], [HAIR], wearing [OUTFIT], [ACTION — standing/sitting/
holding a phone] in [SETTING WITH LIGHTING]. Natural look, [MAKEUP LEVEL], real skin
texture, believable imperfections. Shot on a phone, 9:16 vertical, soft natural light,
high resolution. Not a studio portrait, not overly polished — looks like a real person
about to film a UGC video.
```

Match the avatar to the emotional register of the pain point. **Demographic spread across a campaign:** ages 19–23, 24–32, 32–38, 35–45, plus a male equivalent for at least one variation. Each demographic carries different pressure points — rotate them for algorithm signal and fatigue defense.

Example (from a real run): `generate a candid and natural photo of an attractive woman in her early 40's standing in her modern apartment` — settings: GPT Image 2, High, 2K, 9:16.
